\begin{abstract}
This paper evaluates position- and vision-aided beam prediction using camera
images, GPS position, drone height, and distance information. The results show
that adding geometric sensing information improves position-based prediction,
while the refined image-based model achieves the strongest overall accuracy.
The findings are compared with previously reported results for real-world
drone communication.
\end{abstract}
